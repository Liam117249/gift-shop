About Our Shop

Crafting connections through mindful gift-giving since 2018.

The Gilded Ribbon started as a two-person studio table in the back of an independent printmaking guild. Today, we partner with regional artisans, ceramicists, and textile weavers who share our devotion to durable materials, slow production, and intentional packaging.
